# Widgets

Finance Wizard can sit on your Home Screen. Widgets read the **same data** as the app after a Sync.

| Widget | What it shows | You can edit |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| **Total Spend** | Period total + spend by **card** | Week or month; hide cards in the list |
| **Spend by Category** | Period total + **category** chart | Week or month; bars or pie |
| **Balances** | Checking and savings | — (updates after Sync) |

On **Total Spend**, hiding a card only changes the **breakdown**. The big total still includes every card.

## Add a widget

1. Sync at least once in the app.
2. Home Screen → long-press → **+**
3. Search **Finance Wizard**
4. Choose a widget and a size
5. Long-press → **Edit Widget** for week vs month (where available)

## Empty widgets

| Message | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| Open the app and tap Sync | No data yet — Sync in **Transactions** |
| No spend this week/month | Nothing in that period, or change the widget’s time range |
