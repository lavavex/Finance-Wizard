Budget Magi Support

Help with linking banks, syncing, and Ask.

Contact

Email magisupport@roberth.net. Include your iPhone model and the app version from Settings → About. Please don't send API keys, backup passwords, or bank logins.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. To see how the app works, tap Try with Sample Data on the Welcome screen.

What do I need to link a bank? Your own Plaid API keys, from plaid.com. Add them in Settings, then choose Link bank account.

A bank link failed or timed out. Tap Link bank account and try again, finishing the bank login within about 10 minutes.

Old transactions are missing or look wrong. Tap Sync on the Transactions screen, then choose Full re-sync.

Does Ask send my data anywhere? No. Ask runs on Apple Intelligence on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data? Use Settings → Erase All Data and type DELETE to confirm. It can't be undone, so back up first if you might want the data later.

I got a new phone. In Settings, use Back up everything on the old phone, then restore the file on the new one. Keep the password safe, because it can't be recovered.
